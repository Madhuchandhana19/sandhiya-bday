🎵 BACKGROUND MUSIC INSTRUCTIONS:
==================================
To play your favorite birthday song:
1. Copy your MP3 song file into this "audio/" folder.
2. Rename it to "birthday-song.mp3" (so the full path is audio/birthday-song.mp3).
3. Refresh your website!

Note: If no audio file is provided, the website automatically plays an adorable built-in music-box "Happy Birthday" chime when you click "Play Music"!
