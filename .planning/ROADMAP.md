# Project Roadmap: Sandhiya's 20th Birthday Celebration Website

## Phase Structure (Laptop-Focused Experience)

```text
Phase 1: Audio & Media System Perfection
                  │
                  ▼
Phase 2: Visual Overhaul & Elevated Background Styling (Rich Atmosphere)
                  │
                  ▼
Phase 3: End-to-End Verification & Final Polish
```

---

### Phase 1: Audio & Media System Perfection
* **Goal:** Ensure 100% seamless, resilient audio and media management across the site on desktop/laptop browsers.
* **Scope:**
  * Auto-playback reliability of `audio/birthday-song.mp3` upon login.
  * Mute/unmute sync with header audio pill (`#music-btn`) and visualizer.
  * Clean pause/resume orchestration across Slides 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7.
  * Video player audio unmuting and custom overlay controls.
* **Success Criteria:**
  1. Music plays immediately when "Get Ready ✨" is clicked.
  2. BGM pauses upon reaching Slide 5 (3D cake) and Slide 7 (Video) without audio collision.
  3. Final video plays with audible sound.

---

### Phase 2: Visual Overhaul & Elevated Background Styling
* **Goal:** Transform the visual atmosphere from simple/plain into a stunning, magical, multi-layered celebration aesthetic designed specifically for laptop viewports.
* **Scope:**
  * Replace the simple background with an elevated, rich, atmospheric backdrop (e.g. ambient gradient mesh, floating dreamy bokeh/orbs, glowing star/sparkle canvas, and soft celebratory light ribbons).
  * Enhance card aesthetics (frosted glass/glassmorphism, subtle neon/gold glow borders, refined drop shadows).
  * Enhance Slide 4 (Vintage Scrapbook Letter) with authentic scorched edges, ink drop details, and vintage calligraphy.
  * Polish button micro-interactions, hover glow effects, and celebration confetti.
* **Success Criteria:**
  1. The background feels rich, premium, and alive on desktop/laptop screens without feeling plain or cluttered.
  2. Cards and text maintain high contrast, readability, and visual hierarchy.
  3. Scrapbook letter and interactive elements look handcrafted and visually captivating.

---

### Phase 3: End-to-End Verification & Final Polish
* **Goal:** Full walkthrough from Slide 1 through Slide 7 on laptop browsers with zero errors or glitches.
* **Scope:**
  * Walkthrough of complete experience: Login ➔ Interactive Candle ➔ RPS Game ➔ Vintage Letter ➔ 3D Cake Slicing ➔ Runaway Button ➔ Final Surprise Video.
  * Console error audit, asset check, and performance validation.
* **Success Criteria:**
  1. Smooth progression from start to finish.
  2. No broken links, missing media, or script errors.
